# 06 — Embedded scores and questionnaires

Only names, structure and cut-offs are listed here; full item wording of copyrighted instruments must be licensed or replaced before
shipping (PHQ-9 and GAD-7 are free to use — Pfizer made them available `[APPLE-MHA]`). "Where used" points to the check-in or rule.

## A. Acute / triage scores
| Instrument | What it scores | Inputs | Cut-offs used | Where used | Ref |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **NEWS2** | Early warning of clinical deterioration | RR, SpO₂, air/O₂, SBP, HR, consciousness (ACVPU), temperature | 0–4 low · 5–6 medium (urgent review) · ≥ 7 high (emergency) · any single 3 → urgent | R-CV-01/02, R-RES-*, R-IMM-05 | `[NEWS2]` |
| **qSOFA** (Sepsis-3) | Sepsis risk outside ICU | RR ≥ 22, altered mentation, SBP ≤ 100 | ≥ 2 = high risk | R-IMM-04, R-GU-03 | `[SEPSIS3]` |
| **Glasgow Coma Scale** | Level of consciousness | Eye 1–4, verbal 1–5, motor 1–6 | 15 normal · 13–14 mild · 9–12 moderate · ≤ 8 severe | R-MSK-22/23, R-NEU-12 | `[GCS]` |
| **Canadian CT Head Rule** (elements) | Need for imaging after minor head injury | GCS < 15 at 2 h, suspected skull fracture, vomiting ≥ 2, age ≥ 65, amnesia > 30 min, dangerous mechanism | any high-risk element → RED | R-MSK-22 | `[CCHR]` |
| **BE-FAST** | Stroke recognition | Balance, Eyes, Face, Arm, Speech, Time | any positive → RED, note onset time | R-NEU-07 | `[BEFAST]` |
| **HEART score** (elements) | Chest-pain risk | History, ECG, Age, Risk factors, Troponin | in space: history + ECG elements only; any high-risk feature → RED | R-CV-10 | `[HEART]` |
| **Wells (DVT)** | Pre-test probability of DVT | swelling, tenderness, risk factors… | informs jugular-clot questions (adapted to neck veins [T]) | R-CV-13 | `[WELLS-DVT]` |
| **Orthostatic criteria** | Orthostatic hypotension / tachycardia | BP & HR lying → upright 3 min | SBP ↓ ≥ 20 or DBP ↓ ≥ 10; HR ↑ ≥ 30 | R-CV-07 | `[ORTHO2011]` |
| **Ottawa Ankle Rules** | Need for X-ray after ankle injury | malleolar/midfoot bony tenderness, weight-bearing 4 steps | any → fracture not excluded (weight-bearing only in > 0 g) | R-MSK-05/06 | `[OTTAWA-ANKLE]` |
| **Ottawa Knee Rule** | Need for X-ray after knee injury | age ≥ 55, fibular head / patellar tenderness, cannot flex 90°, cannot bear weight | any → fracture not excluded | R-MSK-07/08 | `[OTTAWA-KNEE]` |
| **Centor criteria** | Bacterial pharyngitis likelihood | fever, tonsillar exudate, tender anterior nodes, no cough | 0–2 likely viral · ≥ 3 consider bacterial | R-ENT-09/10 | `[CENTOR]` |
| **Alvarado score** | Appendicitis likelihood | migration of pain, anorexia, nausea, RLQ tenderness, rebound, fever, leukocytosis, left shift (0–10) | ≥ 7 high · 5–6 equivocal | R-GI-07 | `[ALVARADO]` |
| **STONE score** | Uncomplicated kidney stone likelihood | sex, timing, origin, nausea/vomiting, hematuria (0–13) | high score → stone likely | R-GU-05 | `[STONE]` |
| **Anaphylaxis criteria** | Clinical definition of anaphylaxis | skin/mucosa + respiratory or BP compromise after exposure | criteria met → RED | R-IMM-02 | `[ANAPH]` |
| **Rule of nines** | Burn body-surface area | head 9%, each arm 9%, front/back trunk 18% each, each leg 18%, perineum 1% | ≥ 10% or special areas → RED | R-SKN-08/09 | `[WALLACE]` |
| **SNNOOP10** | Headache red flags | systemic signs, neoplasm history, neuro deficit, onset sudden, older age, pattern change, positional, precipitated by Valsalva, papilledema, progressive, pregnancy, painful eye, post-trauma, immune, overuse | any → secondary headache work-up | R-NEU-04/05 | `[SNNOOP10]` |
| **NRS pain 0–10** | Pain intensity | single item | ≥ 7 severe | all pain rules | `[NRS-PAIN]` |
| **Pensacola Diagnostic Index** | Motion sickness severity | nausea, sweating, pallor, salivation, drowsiness, vomiting… | grading of SMS | R-VES-* | `[PENSACOLA]` |

## B. Sleep, fatigue, workload
| Instrument | Range | Cut-offs | Where used | Ref |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Karolinska Sleepiness Scale (KSS) | 1–9 | ≥ 7 sleepy | morning check-in M3, R-SLP-04 | `[KSS]` |
| Samn-Perelli fatigue scale | 1–7 | ≥ 5 not fit for critical duty | evening E6, R-SLP-06 | `[SAMN-PERELLI]` |
| Psychomotor Vigilance Test (3-min PVT-B) | mean RT, lapses | vs personal baseline (+15% / ×2 lapses [T]) | M7, R-SLP-05 | `[PVT-BASNER]`, `[RST-ISS]` |
| Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index | 0–21 | > 5 poor sleeper | passport H3, P-10 | `[PSQI]` |
| Insomnia Severity Index | 0–28 | 8–14 subthreshold · 15–21 moderate · 22–28 severe | passport H4, P-10 | `[ISI]` |
| Morningness–Eveningness Questionnaire | 16–86 | chronotype groups | passport H2, P-09 | `[MEQ]` |
| Munich ChronoType Questionnaire | mid-sleep on free days | chronotype | passport H1 | `[MCTQ]` |
| Bedford workload scale | 1–10 | ≥ 8 overload [T] | evening E5 | `[BEDFORD]`, `[TB-COGWL]` |
| NASA-TLX | 0–100 (6 subscales) | diagnostic for task design | optional task debrief | `[NASA-TLX]` |

## C. Mood and behavioral health
| Instrument | Range | Cut-offs | Where used | Ref |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| State of Mind valence slider | 1–7 | ≤ 2 unpleasant [T] | evening E1, any-time | `[APPLE-SOM]`, `[CIRCUMPLEX]` |
| PHQ-9 | 0–27 | 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 = mild / moderate / mod-severe / severe; item 9 > 0 → RED | weekly, R-BEH-02/03/09 | `[PHQ9]` |
| GAD-7 | 0–21 | 5 / 10 / 15 = mild / moderate / severe | weekly, R-BEH-04 | `[GAD7]` |
| WHO-5 Well-Being Index | 0–100 | ≤ 50 poor · ≤ 28 likely depression | monthly, R-BEH-12 | `[WHO5]` |
| Perceived Stress Scale (PSS-10) | 0–40 | change vs passport [T] | monthly, R-BEH-13 | `[PSS]` |
| UCLA 3-item loneliness | 3–9 | ≥ 6 lonely | monthly, R-BEH-07 | `[UCLA3]` |
| Big Five (BFI-2-S) | 5 traits | no cut-offs — personalisation only, never triage | passport J1 | `[BFI2]` |
| Single-item self-rated health | 1–7 (our scale) | change vs baseline [T] | morning M4 | `[SRH]` |
